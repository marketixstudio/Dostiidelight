Natural Goodness

The Purity of Our Ingredients

We believe that the best-tasting food is the simplest. That is why Dostii Delight namkeen contains no MSG, no artificial flavor enhancers, and absolutely no synthetic food colors. Our vibrant golden color comes naturally from premium turmeric (haldi), and our rich flavor comes from authentic regional spices.

Our chickpea flour (besan) is milled to a precise consistency, ensuring that the fried sev noodles absorb minimal oil while retaining their signature crispy texture. Our peanuts are slow-roasted in small batches to prevent burning while releasing their natural oils, giving you a healthy dose of plant-based protein and clean energy in every bite.

The Spice Blend

The Art of Traditional Spicing

The heart of Dostii Delight is our proprietary masala blend. Perfected over fifteen years of kitchen trials, this spice mix is a balanced symphony of traditional Indian flavors, combining sweet, sour, salty, and spicy profiles into a single harmonious crunch.

  • Premium Turmeric & Chili: Gives the chivda its warm, golden hue and a pleasant, lingering kick of heat.
  • Amchur (Dry Mango Powder): Adds a delightful tanginess that keeps the palate refreshed.
  • Black Salt (Kala Namak): Provides a deep, savory depth that enhances all other spices.
  • Fresh Curry Leaves: Fried whole to release volatile oils that impart a fragrant, herbal finish.
