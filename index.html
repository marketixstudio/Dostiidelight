Our Farsan Special is crafted using a time-tested, traditional Gujarati and Maharashtrian recipe. We combine crispy gram flour (besan) sev, roasted peanuts, crunchy lentils, and freshly fried curry leaves. Every batch is tossed in our secret signature masala blend, ensuring a perfect balance of savory, spicy, and tangy flavors in every handful.
