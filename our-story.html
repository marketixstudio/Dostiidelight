Our Story

Born from the Joy of Sharing

A namkeen made to be passed around — and remembered.

For over fifteen years, Dostii Delight has crafted namkeen the traditional way — inspired by the rich culinary heritage of Gujarat and Maharashtra. What began as a family recipe, shared among friends and neighbors, has grown into a premium snack brand loved by over a million families across India.

We believe the best moments are shared ones — over hot cups of morning chai, at festive gatherings, or simply during quiet evening snacks. Every batch of our chivda and wafers is made fresh, with pure ingredients and a signature masala blend that carries the warmth of home in every bite.

Our Roots in Phaltan, Maharashtra

Operating from the historic town of Phaltan in the Satara district of Maharashtra, India, Dostii Delight represents the perfect confluence of Western Indian flavors. Our snack recipes capture the perfect balance of Gujarati sweetness and Maharashtrian savory spice, creating a distinctive taste profile that sets us apart.

By sourcing our peanuts, curry leaves, and potatoes directly from local farms in Satara and neighboring districts, we ensure agricultural freshness while supporting regional farmers. We are proud to employ local artisans and women from rural communities, helping build sustainable livelihoods while keeping rich culinary traditions alive.

Crafted Without Compromise

Our dedication to quality goes beyond just taste. In a world full of mass-produced, chemically-enhanced snacks, Dostii Delight remains committed to natural purity. All our products are 100% vegetarian, FSSAI certified, and completely free from MSG, artificial flavorings, and synthetic food colors.

From the rigorous selection of raw chickpea flour (besan) to the custom roasting of peanuts and the final nitrogen-flushed packaging that keeps moisture out, we maintain high standards of quality and hygiene. Snacking is an act of trust, and we honor that trust with every package we seal.
