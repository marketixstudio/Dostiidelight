Our Story

Born from the Joy of Sharing

A namkeen made to be passed around — and remembered.

Dostii Delight began not in a factory, but in a home kitchen in Dhuldev — a small, close-knit village in Phaltan Taluka, Satara District, Maharashtra. It was here that Mr. Dilip Choramale, the company's founder and Chairman, first began crafting namkeen the way his elders taught him: slow-roasted, hand-seasoned, and made with a generosity of spirit that no machine can replicate.

Driven by a simple but powerful belief — that the best food brings people together — Mr. Choramale built Dostii Delight from the ground up, turning a village tradition into a brand that today reaches over a million families across India. His inspiration was the word Dostii itself: friendship, warmth, and the joy of sharing something truly good with the people you love.

From Dhuldev to Every Doorstep

What started in one village in the Satara district has grown into a legacy of quality and trust. Dostii Delight sources its peanuts, curry leaves, and produce directly from local farms in and around Dhuldev, ensuring every batch carries the authentic flavour of the land it comes from — while supporting the farming families of the region.

Our snacks capture the perfect balance of Gujarati sweetness and Maharashtrian savoury spice — a confluence born naturally from our roots, where both culinary traditions have long lived side by side. That distinctive taste profile, perfected over fifteen years, is what sets Dostii Delight apart.

Crafted Without Compromise

Our dedication to quality goes beyond taste. In a world full of mass-produced, chemically-enhanced snacks, Dostii Delight remains committed to natural purity. All our products are 100% vegetarian, FSSAI certified, and completely free from MSG, artificial flavourings, and synthetic food colours.

From the careful milling of chickpea flour (besan) to the custom roasting of peanuts in small batches, and the final nitrogen-flushed packaging that locks in freshness — every step is carried out with the same care Mr. Choramale brought to his very first batch. Snacking is an act of trust, and we honour that trust with every package we seal.
