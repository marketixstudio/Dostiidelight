Premium Quality

The Dostii Quality Standard

At Dostii Delight, we are committed to keeping traditional Indian snacking alive through pure, high-quality ingredients and uncompromising hygiene standards. We source our raw materials directly from select regional farms in Maharashtra and Gujarat, ensuring that only the finest gram flour (besan), golden peanuts, raw bananas, and aromatic spices enter our kitchen.

Our recipes combine generations-old Maharashtrian and Gujarati food heritage. Each product, from our signature Farsan Special to our crisp Banana Wafers, is prepared in small, carefully monitored batches. This process allows us to control the oil absorption, preserve the natural flavors, and lock in that bakery-fresh, golden crunch you love.

Serving Suggestions

Pairings & Occasions

Dostii Delight snacks are designed for sharing and bringing people together. Whether you are hosting a festive gathering, packing a snack box for a road trip, or simply enjoying your evening cup of hot masala chai, our chivda and wafers add the perfect crunch to the moment.

  • Chai Time Companion: The perfect savory counterpoint to hot sweet tea or filter coffee.
  • Street Food Enhancer: Sprinkle our Farsan Special or Sev Chivda over Misal Pav, Bhel Puri, or homemade chaats for an authentic crunch.
  • Festive Celebrations: A staple snack for Diwali, Ganesh Chaturthi, Holi, and family gatherings.
  • Upvas (Fasting) Delights: Enjoy our Potato Chivda and Sabudana Chivda during religious fasts for a satisfying and energy-boosting treat.
